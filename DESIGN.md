# StudySnap — Linear Slate (warm-dark)

Linear/Vercel/Stripe aesthetic but warm-toned. Warm near-black background, peach CTA, Inter throughout.

## Tokens
- **Surface**: #1A1714 warm near-black · **Card**: #25201C
- **Primary**: #FFB088 peach (NOT blue) · **Border**: #3D362F warm-brown
- **Display + Body**: Inter (Inter Variable preferred)
- **Radii**: 10px · **Shadows**: none, only 1px borders

## Why this works for grades 1-8
For 6yo: warm color avoids harshness of pure dark mode.
For 13yo: looks like a sophisticated developer tool — premium, not babyish.

## Lyttle onboarding hook
Welcome: minimal, confident — "Build your child's study practice." Each step is a clean card with a 1px border.
