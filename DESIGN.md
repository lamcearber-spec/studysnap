# StudySnap — Backpack & Trail

Vintage scout handbook reimagined. Kraft tan + forest green + burnt orange + cream cardstock. Stitched dashed borders. Topographic line accents.

## Tokens
- **Surface**: #E8D4A8 kraft tan · **Card**: #FBF4E3 cream
- **Forest**: #2C4A2E (primary) · **Burnt orange**: #C8581A · **Olive**: #7A8B53
- **Borders**: 1px dashed white "stitch" inside cards
- **Display**: Epilogue (Literata substitute) · **Body**: Inter

## Why this works for grades 1-8
For 6yo: campfire/patches/trail motifs are universally appealing.
For 13yo: outdoor-adventure aesthetic is mature and high-quality (think Patagonia / REI).

## Lyttle onboarding hook
Welcome: "Map your child's learning trail." Personalization questions appear as merit-badge milestones along a dashed trail.
