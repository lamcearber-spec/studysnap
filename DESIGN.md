# StudySnap — Field Notes Stationery

Kraft paper, ink-blue, hand-stamped red. The app feels like a durable academic notebook tucked in a canvas satchel.

## Tokens
- **Surface**: #E8D4A8 kraft paper · **Card**: #FBF4E3 cream
- **Ink**: #1A2B4F navy · **Accent**: #C73E2C stamp red
- **Display**: Libre Caslon Text · **Body**: Inter

## Why this works for grades 1-8
For 6yo: tactile metaphors (paper, stamps, polaroids) are intuitive.
For 13yo: durable scholarly aesthetic avoids "babyish" tropes — feels like a real tool.

## Lyttle onboarding hook
Welcome: "Help your child build a daily study habit." Stamps and tape across the welcome flow. Each personalization step displays as a fresh Field Notes-style page.
