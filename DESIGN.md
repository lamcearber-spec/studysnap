# StudySnap — Risograph Workbook

Flat ink overprint, halftone dots, 2px black borders, sticker chips. Like a beloved school zine.

## Tokens
- **Surface**: #FBF6EC newsprint · **Card**: #FFFFFF
- **Riso Red**: #FF4500 · **Ultramarine**: #1A50C8 (secondary) · **Yellow**: #FFD42E
- **Borders**: 2px solid black always
- **Display**: Epilogue Bold (Anybody substitute) · **Body**: Inter

## Why this works for grades 1-8
For 6yo: bold primary-adjacent colors and sticker chips read as playful.
For 13yo: streetwear-zine aesthetic feels cool, not corny. Retro-print is currently in.

## Lyttle onboarding hook
Welcome: bold riso-red banner "BUILD YOUR CHILD'S WORKBOOK." Each personalization step is a halftone-dotted card.
