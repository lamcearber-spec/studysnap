# StudySnap — Tokyo Stationery

MUJI / Itoya / Hobonichi. Off-white paper, pure black ink, single vermillion red accent. 1px hairlines. Extreme whitespace. The opposite of "kid app."

## Tokens
- **Surface**: #FAFAF7 paper · **Ink**: #0F0F0F
- **Vermillion**: #D63B2C (USED ONLY for primary CTA + brand mark + section dots)
- **Borders**: 1px hairlines only · **Cards**: do not exist (sections separated by rules)
- **Display + Body**: Epilogue (Hanken Grotesk substitute) + Inter

## Why this works for grades 1-8
For 6yo: extreme clarity and removal of visual noise = easier to parse.
For 13yo: most "premium-feeling" of all directions — feels like an adult tool that respects them.

## Lyttle onboarding hook
Welcome: tiny red dot + minimal headline "Your child. Their study. Quiet, daily." Each Lyttle question on its own page with massive whitespace + a single red dot for "Next."
