# StudySnap — Mid-Century Modern

1950s-60s Eames + IBM annual report. Saturated cream + mustard + teal + brick red. Flat geometric blocks. Type wide-tracked.

## Tokens
- **Surface**: #F4ECD8 cream · **Card**: #FBF4E0
- **Teal**: #1F6E7A (primary) · **Mustard**: #E8B339 · **Brick**: #B8423B
- **Display**: Epilogue Bold (Montserrat substitute, +0.03em tracking)
- **Radii**: 0px sharp

## Why this works for grades 1-8
For 6yo: bold, confident colors register as "important" without being childish.
For 13yo: vintage IBM look is "smart" — feels like a tool designed by adults.
