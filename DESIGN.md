# StudySnap — Library Cardroom

Yale common room. Dark walnut, aged ivory cards, brass hairlines, oxblood accent. Playfair Display.

## Tokens
- **Surface**: #3D2A1F walnut · **Card**: #F5EBD7 ivory paper
- **Brass**: #B89968 · **Oxblood**: #7B1F2A · **Forest**: #2D4A3E
- **Display**: Playfair Display 700 · **Body**: Inter (oldstyle nums)

## Why this works for grades 1-8
For 6yo: rich textures (walnut, brass, ivory) are sensorily rewarding.
For 13yo: aspirational maturity — "I'm using grown-up software."

## Lyttle onboarding hook
Welcome: "Welcome to your child's reading room." Each step looks like a leather-bound library card.
