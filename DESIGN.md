# StudySnap — Origami Paper

Washi paper philosophy. Cool pastel palette on warm paper-white. Soft layered drop shadows. Folded-corner motifs.

## Tokens
- **Surface**: #F4F1E8 paper-white · **Card**: #FFFFFF
- **Pastels**: Sky #B8DCFF · Sakura #FFC9D6 · Mint #C4F0D7 · Butter #FFE8A8
- **Display**: Epilogue (Plus Jakarta substitute) · **Body**: Inter
- **Shadows**: 0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.04) — ultra-soft

## Why this works for grades 1-8
For 6yo: soft pastels and paper-fold motifs are calming.
For 13yo: tactile minimalism feels intelligent and curated, not childish.
