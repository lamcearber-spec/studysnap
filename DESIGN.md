# StudySnap — Editorial Broadsheet

Cream + deep burgundy + huge serif. 1px hairlines instead of cards. Headlines do the talking.

## Tokens
- **Surface**: #F8F1E4 cream · **Ink**: #1A1A1A · **Burgundy**: #8B1A1A
- **Display**: Newsreader 700 (serif) · **Body**: Inter
- **Borders**: 1px hairlines · **Radii**: 0px

## Why this works for grades 1-8
For 6yo: clear typographic hierarchy aids parsing; warm cream is non-clinical.
For 13yo: the "serious newspaper" aesthetic respects them as readers.

## Lyttle onboarding hook
Welcome: huge serif headline "FRONT PAGE OF YOUR CHILD'S YEAR." Each step formatted like a newspaper article with kicker + headline + dek.
