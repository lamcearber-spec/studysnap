# StudySnap — Cosmic Arcade

Soft pastels on cosmic indigo — dark mode. 2px pixel borders. Sticker-chip subjects in pastel violet/pink/mint/lemon.

## Tokens
- **Surface**: #161435 cosmic indigo · **Card**: #1F1D40
- **Pastels**: Violet #B6A3FF · Pink #FFB3D1 · Mint #9FE5C4 · Lemon #FFE89F
- **Borders**: 2px solid · **Press shadow**: 3px hard offset
- **Display**: Epilogue (VT323 reserved for stat numbers) · **Body**: Inter

## Why this works for grades 1-8
For 6yo: chunky tactile buttons + bright pastels are inviting.
For 13yo: 8-bit nostalgia is "cool" right now — references gaming culture without infantilizing.

## Lyttle onboarding hook
Welcome: "PRESS START" framing. Each personalization step shows pixel chips. Score-chip numerals on the progress bar.
